# 05. The Lotuses Not Yet Open

Source anchor: SN6.1, Pali Canon, Theravada transmission.

## Narrative scene
Brahma respectfully requests teaching from the newly awakened Buddha.

## Art direction
Medium treatment: Buddhist influenced gouache; AI-generated simulation, not a physical original painting. Portrait composition, meaningful restrained gesture and nuanced expression. Indigo, teal, ivory, ochre and earth; natural light. South Asian people, ancient north Indian landscape, simple draped cloth, clay vessels and modest rural structures. No modern objects or indiscriminate East Asian/Tibetan motifs.

Character continuity: adult Buddha with gentle South Asian features, tightly curled dark hair and ochre robe; the pre-awakening seeker is lean with simple hair. The final journey uses an elderly face. These are devotional artistic conventions, not a recovered historical likeness. Avoid jewelry, excessive luminosity and ornamental spectacle.

Composition and gesture: stage the stated encounter clearly; give each person a distinct expression and credible hands. Parable and conceptual scenes are illustrations of teachings rather than claims that the depicted encounter literally took place. Brahma/Mara belong to the canonical religious narrative. Scenery and props not specified by the source are artistic reconstruction.
