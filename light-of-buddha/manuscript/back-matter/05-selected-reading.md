# Bibliography

## Text Repositories and Verse Editions

**SuttaCentral.** Pali root texts, translations and parallels for the canonical discourses cited in the individual source notes. https://suttacentral.net/

**Ancient Buddhist Texts.** Ānandajoti Bhikkhu’s *Dhammapada: A New Edition*, including readings and variant notes. The original-language chapters were used to check the Pali interludes. https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Buddhist-Texts/K2-Dhammapada-New/index.htm

**Dhammatalks.org.** Thanissaro Bhikkhu’s *Khuddakapatha*, especially Khp 1, “Going for Refuge”. Consulted for the identity and liturgical structure of the refuge passage; its modern translation is not reproduced as a whole. https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/KN/Khp/

**Sutta Friends.** Khp 1, “Saraṇagamanaṁ”. Pali refuge formula and translation for comparison. https://suttafriends.org/sutta/khp1/

A story-by-story verification register accompanies the production project. These repositories contain distinct works under distinct copyright or licensing terms. Availability online is not equivalent to permission for commercial reproduction.
