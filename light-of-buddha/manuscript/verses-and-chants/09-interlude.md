# Interlude 9: A Reliable Guardian

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Attā hi attano nātho, ko hi nātho paro siyā?  
Attanā va sudantena nāthaṃ labhati dullabhaṃ.

## English Meaning

You are your own guardian: who else could be such a guardian?  
Through training yourself well, you find a protection difficult to obtain.

## Context and Significance

The conventional language of “self” expresses responsibility in practice. It is not a declaration of an eternal soul and does not deny the importance of teachers and mutual care.
