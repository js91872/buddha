# Interlude 5: The Ending of Hostility

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Na hi verena verāni sammantīdha kudācanaṃ;  
Averena ca sammanti, esa dhammo sanantano.

## English Meaning

Hostilities never settle through hostility here.  
Through non-hostility they settle: this is an enduring principle.

## Context and Significance

Avera literally points to the absence of hostility. This verse supports the refusal to feed hatred; it does not require remaining in an unsafe situation or abandoning necessary protection.
