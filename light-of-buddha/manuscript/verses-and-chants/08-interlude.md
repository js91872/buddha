# Interlude 8: The Greater Victory

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Yo sahassaṃ sahassena saṅgāme mānuse jine,  
Ekañca jeyya attānaṃ, sa ve saṅgāmajuttamo.

## English Meaning

Someone might conquer a thousand times a thousand people in battle.  
The person who conquers just one—themselves—is the greatest victor.

## Context and Significance

The battle image is redirected towards mastery of harmful impulses. It is not an invitation to treat one’s body or emotions as enemies.
