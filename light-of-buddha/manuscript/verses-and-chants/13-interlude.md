# Interlude 13: Seeing Change Clearly

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā ti, yadā paññāya passati,  
Atha nibbindatī dukkhe—esa maggo visuddhiyā.

## English Meaning

When one sees with wisdom that all conditioned formations are impermanent,  
one grows disenchanted with suffering: this is the path to purification.

## Context and Significance

Saṅkhārā here means conditioned formations, not simply every thing without qualification. Disenchantment loosens fascination and clinging; it is not contempt for life.
