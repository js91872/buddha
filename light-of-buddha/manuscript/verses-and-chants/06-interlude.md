# Interlude 6: The Work Before Us

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Na paresaṃ vilomāni, na paresaṃ katākataṃ;  
Attano va avekkheyya katāni akatāni ca.

## English Meaning

Do not dwell on others’ faults, or on what they have and have not done.  
Look instead at your own actions and omissions.

## Context and Significance

The verse redirects scrutiny towards personal responsibility. It does not forbid recognising injustice or holding harmful conduct accountable.
