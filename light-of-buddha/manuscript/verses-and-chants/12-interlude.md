# Interlude 12: The Wealth of Enough

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Ārogyaparamā lābhā, santuṭṭhiparamaṃ dhanaṃ;  
Vissāsaparamā ñātī, nibbānaṃ paramaṃ sukhaṃ.

## English Meaning

Health stands foremost among gains; contentment among riches.  
Trust is the finest kinship; nibbana is the highest happiness.

## Context and Significance

This praise of health and contentment is a value statement, not a judgement on people living with illness or poverty. Liberation remains distinct from ordinary comfort.
