# Interlude 4: Training What Wanders

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Dunniggahassa lahuno yatthakāmanipātino,  
Cittassa damatho sādhu, cittaṃ dantaṃ sukhāvahaṃ.

## English Meaning

The mind is hard to hold, quick, alighting wherever it wishes.  
It is good to train it: a trained mind brings happiness.

## Context and Significance

Training here concerns cultivation, not hatred of one’s thoughts. The verse is an invitation to learn how attention and intention behave.
