# Interlude 1: A Direction for the Heart

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.  
Dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.  
Saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.

## English Meaning

To the Buddha I turn for refuge.  
To the Dhamma I turn for refuge.  
To the Saṅgha I turn for refuge.

## Context and Significance

The first cycle of the Three Refuges. Khp 1 repeats the commitment a second and third time. In Theravada practice, refuge is a conscious orientation towards awakening, the teaching and the community of noble disciples. These three lines are an excerpt, not the full nine-line recitation.
