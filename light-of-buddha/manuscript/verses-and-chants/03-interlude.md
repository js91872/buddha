# Interlude 3: A Care That Does Not Sleep

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Appamādo amatapadaṃ, pamādo maccuno padaṃ;  
Appamattā na mīyanti, ye pamattā yathā matā.

## English Meaning

Heedfulness is the way to the Deathless; heedlessness is the way to death.  
The heedful do not die; the heedless are as though already dead.

## Context and Significance

“Deathless” belongs to the Buddhist language of liberation. The verse does not promise that a careful person’s physical body will never die. Heedfulness is sustained ethical and contemplative care.
