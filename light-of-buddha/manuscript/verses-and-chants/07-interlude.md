# Interlude 7: A Rock in the Wind

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Selo yathā ekaghano vātena na samīrati,  
Evaṃ nindāpasaṃsāsu na samiñjanti paṇḍitā.

## English Meaning

As a solid rock is not stirred by the wind,  
so the wise are not shaken by blame and praise.

## Context and Significance

Equanimity is steadiness amid changing evaluations. It can coexist with listening carefully to useful criticism.
