# Interlude 11: Patience Without Injury

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Khantī paramaṃ tapo titikkhā,  
Nibbānaṃ paramaṃ vadanti buddhā;  
Na hi pabbajito parūpaghātī,  
Samaṇo hoti paraṃ viheṭhayanto.

## English Meaning

Patient endurance is the highest austerity;  
the Buddhas call nibbana supreme.  
One who harms others is no true renunciant;  
one who torments others is no contemplative.

## Context and Significance

This canonical verse links spiritual discipline to non-injury. Its praise of endurance cannot justify abuse inflicted by a teacher or anyone else.
