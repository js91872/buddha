# Interlude 10: The Shape of the Teaching

## Pali — Roman Transliteration

Sabbapāpassa akaraṇaṃ, kusalassa upasampadā;  
Sacittapariyodapanaṃ, etaṃ buddhāna sāsanaṃ.

## English Meaning

Refrain from every harmful deed; bring what is skilful to fulfilment;  
clear your own mind: this is the instruction of the Buddhas.

## Context and Significance

The verse joins restraint, positive cultivation and purification of mind. The plural “Buddhas” belongs to Buddhist tradition and should not be silently changed to a single speaker’s modern slogan.
