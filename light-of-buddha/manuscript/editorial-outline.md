## Editorial safeguards

- Do not import the chariot's four sights, Sujata's named offering, or a midnight horse departure into the early canonical accounts that do not narrate them.
- The rose-apple memory in MN36 precedes recovery and awakening; do not merge it with a historical scene beneath the Bodhi tree.
- MN21 includes disturbing similes: avoid decorative violence while preserving the demanding teaching about anger.
- MN86 records both radical ethical transformation and continuing consequences; forgiveness does not erase harm.
- SN5.2 challenges the claim that women cannot awaken; do not portray Soma as depending on a male rescuer.
- AN3.65 does not teach that every private opinion is equally sound; ethical consequences and the judgement of the wise matter.
- SN3.14 ends with defeat breeding suffering; do not import the later battle and release from SN3.15.
- SN47.19 acrobat gender differs between translations; avoid treating an English rendering as historical proof.
- DN16 contains layered religious traditions; neither the final meal's precise ingredient nor a modern medical cause of death is established.
