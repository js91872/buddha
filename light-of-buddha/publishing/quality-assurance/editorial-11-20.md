2. **MN58 infant posture corrected.** The child now lies on the prince’s lap rather than sitting. The illustration brief simply holds the infant safely, which remains compatible.
3. **Literary flow strengthened.** Reduced repeated in-narrative phrases such as “the canonical account” and moved the burden of classification to Source Notes. Revised paragraphs in 13, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 20 retain their meaning while sustaining the scene. The Angulimala opening now uses the source’s cowherds, shepherds and farmers rather than a generic warning crowd.
4. **Ethical difficulty retained.** Angulimala’s victims and lasting consequences remain present. No legal immunity is implied. The supernatural pursuit is still expressly identified as a miracle, and the Source Note cautions against medical claims for the childbirth blessing.
5. **Interpretation distinguished.** MN93’s flame is hypothetical. MN22’s traveller is a parable. MN58’s infant does not actually choke. SN5.2’s Mara remains the canonical adversary, with psychological interpretation identified only as an option in the note. The Kalama teaching retains wise counsel and observable consequences, rather than becoming an endorsement of preference.
