## Overall observations

The images share warm ochre/earth colours, composed faces and fine-art surface treatment. Individual scene staging is generally legible. This visual coherence also creates repetition in Buddha’s robe and face, and the first batch wrongly imported him into19 and20. Those are substantive source-fidelity issues, not matters to conceal through a generic art disclaimer.

No definite gross extra-limb or extra-finger anomaly was identified among11–20 at this inspection scale. That finding is limited; it does not certify every small digit, costume detail or architectural element. Images11,13,14 contain varied cloth and rural scenery; no indiscriminate Chinese, Japanese or Tibetan architecture was seen. Image13’s tower remains the clear historical concern.

Briefs11,15,16,17,18,19,20 now include scene-specific caveats. No image editing or generation was performed by this reviewing agent.
