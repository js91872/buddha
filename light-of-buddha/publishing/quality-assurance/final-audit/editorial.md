## Corrections actually applied

- **11:** Replaced the unsupported claim that the parable traveller had “no village to return to” with the accurate observation that the parable supplies no name or family history.
- **16:** Changed “Every journey for food” to “His alms rounds” to avoid an unsupported universal claim about his later movements. The already-corrected sequence remains childbirth blessing, attainment, then assault.
- **17:** Changed “servant and master” to “an enslaved person and a master” for the MN93 example of differing social arrangements. This avoids euphemising the ancient category.
- **18:** Recast the six-elements sentence as examination of human experience, rather than describing consciousness as a property of the material body. Preserved all six categories and the existing caveat that they are contemplative categories, not modern physics.
- **19:** Removed a redundant paragraph beginning “Either way” whose alternative had disappeared in an earlier edit. The removal fixes an orphan transition and reduces repetition while retaining Soma’s full encounter and response.
- **26:** Changed a categorical “The body would age” into being subject to ageing and illness, closer to the recollection’s meaning and without implying every life reaches old age.
- **02,05,09:** Standardised “judgment” to “judgement”. Existing British/international forms such as practising, recognised, towards, ageing and skilful were retained.

A unified diff against the preserved before-final-audit snapshot is saved as editorial-changes.diff. No edits to credits, artwork, metadata or build files were made by this editorial reviewer.
