# Final individual artwork audit: illustrations 16–30

Audit date: 23 October 2026. Reviewer: AI visual/editorial agent. This is an actual new visual inspection, not a restatement of the earlier contact-sheet reports; it is not human art-historical or physical-print approval.

## Inspection performed

Opened each final JPEG individually with `tools.view_image`, `detail: "original"`, in three batches of five: 16–20, 21–25, and 26–30. Every image was displayed and inspected at its complete native dimensions, including visible faces, hands, feet, proportions, costume, scene construction, and picture edges. Read the relevant manuscript scene or source note where an interpretive concern arose. No contact sheet substituted for an individual viewing.

Also ran a Python/Pillow decoding check for each file using `Image.open(path).verify()`, then read dimensions, colour mode, byte size, and stored DPI. All fifteen decoded successfully. Every file is RGB JPEG, 1122 × 1402 pixels, with 300-DPI metadata. Stored DPI does not increase native detail.

## Cross-image assessment

- **Buddha character:** recognisable continuity in adult South Asian facial type, dark curls/ushnisha convention, elongated ears and ochre robe across16–18/21–22; older treatment in28 and30. Exact facial identity is not identical in every painting and is a devotional construction, not a recovered likeness. No broad regeneration is justified by that normal illustrated-book variation.
- **Anatomy:** no gross extra/missing limb, detached hand, severely distorted main face or obvious duplicated digit was found in these fifteen native-size inspections. Naturally obscured digits were not treated as errors. This is a bounded visual finding, not a claim of anatomical perfection.
- **Historical/religious care:** no obvious cross-tradition iconographic collage, ornamental fantasy architecture, inscriptions pretending to be ancient artefacts, or modern machinery. Some garment and scene choices are reconstructions. Particular limits for18,26,28 and29 are stated above.
- **Artistic variety:** oil-like depth/chiaroscuro in16,18,23,28; lighter watercolour-like texture and open compositions in19,21–22,24,26–27,29; a sober narrative procession in25 and devotional reclining composition in30. The earthy ochre/ivory/indigo palette ties them together. This subset does not demonstrate a separate pencil/ink medium, and should not be advertised as a set of physical handmade paintings.
- **Print resolution:** 1122-pixel width yields 300ppi at3.74in, 280.5ppi at4in, and approximately249ppi at4.5in. These are reasonable moderate-size interior images, not native300ppi full-page six-inch-wide artwork. For a strict300ppi production requirement, keep image width at or below3.74in or obtain genuinely higher-resolution sources. Do not solve this by changing DPI metadata alone. Colour and shadow detail still require a physical print proof.
- **Cropping/edges:** no central face or essential gesture is accidentally clipped; intentional peripheral figure/edge cropping in20/30 does not obscure the scene's meaning. No image-generated lettering defect is present because these paintings contain no typography.

## Result

All fifteen final illustrations were inspected individually and can be retained. No replacement was generated, no source artwork overwritten, and no satisfactory existing image regenerated during this audit. Main outstanding art limitations are physical-print proofing, native resolution at large reproduction sizes, reconstructed historical details, and the non-identical Pasenadi portraits in20/25. They should be documented honestly rather than used to justify wholesale regeneration.
