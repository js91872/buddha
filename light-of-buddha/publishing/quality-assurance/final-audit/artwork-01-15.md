## Character consistency and artistic range

The adult Buddha is recognisable across 04–08, 10, and 12–15 through a warm South Asian complexion, gentle oval face, dark curls, elongated ears and ochre/saffron drapery. There is modest variation in apparent age, facial breadth and prominence of the cranial form; this remains a coherent devotional identity rather than a precisely controlled photographic likeness. The younger seeker in 01/03 and the child in 02 are deliberately differentiated. The physical historical Buddha’s precise face, robe colour, hairstyle and appearance are not known from contemporary portraits.

Most images share a detailed ochre/green/blue painterly finish. Image 03 is a clear line-art exception, 04 is a darker oil-like treatment, and 11 has a lighter watercolor-like landscape. Teaching compositions 06/07/10 are similar; the collection is coherent but its declared range of media should not be overstated as fifteen wholly different painting techniques. No visible copyrighted signature, false ancient inscription, watermark, image-generated lettering, plastic-looking face or excessive magical lighting was detected in these fifteen.

## Resolution and print limits

All fifteen final JPEGs are RGB, **1122 × 1402 pixels**, decoded successfully, and range from 788,117 to 1,119,482 bytes. At the current build script’s 4-inch image width, effective native resolution is **280.5 PPI**, with height approximately 5 inches. That is suitable for a credible review proof and near conventional 300 PPI practice, but it is below a strict 300-PPI target. At 300 PPI the native files should be displayed at no more than **3.74 × 4.67 inches**. Enlarging, adding a DPI tag, or upsampling will not create additional painted detail. If 300-PPI print production is mandatory, reduce placed image width to 3.74 inches or obtain genuinely larger source files; do not claim an upsampled replacement restores detail. Kindle use is technically adequate at these dimensions. A physical colour proof remains necessary to judge shadow loss in 04, skin tone, fine graphite detail in 03 and paper-specific colour.

## Necessary action recommendation

No wholesale replacement is justified by this inspection. Preserve all original assets. The source-to-image discrepancy in 05 should be resolved editorially as an explicitly nonliteral devotional rendering, or by one narrowly scoped new edit after root’s decision; no claim of a corrected image is made here. Retaining interpretive art requires readers not to be told that its posture, foliage, objects and likeness are documentary reconstructions. Existing alt text broadly describes the scenes without claiming exact botanical or historical detail.
