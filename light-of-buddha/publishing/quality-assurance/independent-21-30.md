# Independent manuscript-to-source review: stories 21–30

Date: 23 October 2026. Reviewer: a separate AI research/editorial agent, not the drafting agent and not a human Buddhist scholar. This is English-source fidelity review, not full Pali philological collation or legal certification.

## Method

Read each manuscript file, including its source note and reflection, against the canonical narrative anchors in live English source pages. Relevant passages were actually retrieved; no frontend JavaScript shell counted as a text check. Source links below identify the research editions, whose modern English is not reused as book prose.

## Scope and residual limits

Review checks source fidelity and attribution, not historicity. Original narrative interpretation and modern reflections are not canonical quotations. No claim of human scholarly review, complete comparative-canon work, or licensed copying of ATI's noncommercial translation is made. Automated structural and visual checks are separate.
