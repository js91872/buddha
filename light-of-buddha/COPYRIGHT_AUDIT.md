# Copyright and provenance audit

Status: production audit for human approval, not legal clearance or a claim of human authorship.

Ancient canonical narratives and Pali wording are distinguished from modern translated expression. Original retellings and editorial prose have been generated for this project. Research access does not imply permission to reproduce modern translations. Do not copy modern translation wording beyond permitted/licensed uses. Source-specific translation permissions and any adapted renderings must be checked against the research register before release.

Cover and story art generated for this project are identified as AI-generated. They are not museum photographs, commissioned human paintings, or ancient artefacts. No signature-style imitation of a living artist is intended. Detailed art briefs, generated masters, final exports and provenance records are retained with the project. The complete raw generation-prompt history remains in the conversation rather than a separate verbatim prompt log. The briefs support maintenance and review; deterministic recreation of generated images is not claimed. The presence of files alone does not establish that every image is anatomically or culturally flawless; see ARTWORK_REVIEW.md.

The user-supplied compiler name and exact credit are preserved. No personal biography has been invented. A compiler credit does not establish copyright ownership of every element. Copyright eligibility and ownership of AI-generated content vary by jurisdiction, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and India. Avoid claiming exclusive copyright over unprotectable AI output or ancient material; publisher review of any copyright notice is required.

Font license evidence is retained in publishing/metadata/font-licenses/. Cover raster typography uses Nimbus Roman and DejaVu Sans; the print pipeline embeds Noto Serif. Review the installed license notices for exact attribution/redistribution terms. Raster cover lettering does not distribute font software. The EPUB uses reader-selected generic fonts and does not embed font binaries.

AI disclosure: KDP's official Content Guidelines require disclosure of generated text, images and translations even after substantial editing. This project includes generated prose and images. Use the appropriate generated-text/image/translation options during final setup. Do not describe the book as solely human authored or the art as hand painted.

Release restrictions: no KDP submission or public publication is authorised by the build. Publisher must confirm rights, territory, contributor role, final notices and disclosure before upload. No unsupported claim that a copyright lawyer, Buddhist scholar or professional human proofreader has reviewed this package is made.
