B selected and regenerated as a standalone 5:8 image with greater title space and a darker lower edge for the compiler credit. The final title and all other text are composited with actual fonts in scripts/cover.py, not generated inside the painting. Text checked against the supplied exact strings. The 200×320 thumbnail is included for human review; the main title and compiler credit remain visible, while the small subtitle requires a larger view, as usual at this size.

The painting uses later devotional conventions (ushnisha, curled hair, urna); it is not a claim to reconstruct Gautama's historical appearance. Its watercolour treatment is AI generated, not a physical watercolour or a human illustrator's work. Original source 992×1586 pixels; the marketing output is resized to 1600×2560. Upsampling does not create native detail. A higher native-resolution commissioned or regenerated master is desirable for large physical uses. No print full wrap has been fabricated.
