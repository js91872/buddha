# Artwork review — 23 October 2026

## Inventory and direction

Thirty individually generated story paintings/drawings, thirty retained native PNG masters, thirty optimised JPEGs, thirty art briefs and separate alt-text records are provided. The selected cover has a retained standalone master and separately composited typography. Three distinct cover treatments and a typographic comparison are included. No museum image or other third-party artwork was used as an input or embedded asset.

The collection uses a restrained indigo, teal, ivory and ochre palette. Watercolour and gouache treatments are balanced by a graphite/sepia drawing (03), dark tonal paintings (04,18,23) and oil treatments (28,30). These are AI-generated simulations of media, not physical paintings or works created by human fine artists. Traditional devotional hair/marks are artistic conventions; they are not evidence of Gautama's historical appearance. Paintings of the earlier seeker remain idealised. Settings, garment details, bowls and plants are informed reconstructions, not archaeological facsimiles.

## Actual visual review

All thirty initial images were inspected through contact sheets; stories 11–20 also received individual-image inspection by a separate AI agent. Corrected images and selected high-detail originals were reopened. This establishes an actual AI visual review, not human art-director certification or pixel-by-pixel proof of perfect anatomy. Faces, gestures, hands, posture, contextual buildings and scene identity were considered. No definite major extra limb or digit remains identified in the retained images at the inspected scale; subtle AI rendering faults may still require human correction.

## Resolution and cropping

Native masters are preserved, generally 1122×1402 pixels. Story art is not cropped. The final-audit print edition places it at 3.74 inches width, giving 300 ppi from the native 1122-pixel width. Earlier 4-inch placements provided approximately 280 ppi. A 300 dpi JPEG tag does not add native detail. A physical colour proof remains required. No higher native resolution is claimed. The cover master is 992×1586 pixels, resized to the requested 1600×2560 RGB JPEG; this meets the requested pixel dimensions but does not create genuine higher-resolution detail. Higher-resolution source art remains desirable, especially for any larger print reuse.

The front cover was checked at full size and 200×320 thumbnail. All exact text is typeset with real fonts. The title remains readable at thumbnail size; small subtitle text needs a larger view. Source art, text file, reusable typography scripts and font-licence evidence are retained. No paperback full-wrap cover was prepared because final paper/bleed/production specifications are unconfirmed.
